Meet Chef Mohnish Malhotra

Born in New Delhi and shaped by a lifelong curiosity for food, Chef Mohnish brings a personal perspective to Indian cuisine in Puerto Vallarta.

For him, cooking begins with the senses: aroma, flavour, texture and the memories food can awaken. At Purpose, familiar traditions meet personal expression, with a focus on pleasure, generosity and discovery.
