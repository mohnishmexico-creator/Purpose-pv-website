Indian Food Menu in Puerto Vallarta

Welcome to Purpose PV, an Indian restaurant in Versalles, Puerto Vallarta. Our menu brings together the warmth of Indian home-style cooking, aromatic spices, tandoor favourites and freshly prepared breads. Explore the menu below or open the complete menu PDF.

Tandoor & Starters

Begin with Indian starters and tandoor favourites, prepared to bring out the depth of spice, aroma and texture.

Indian Curries

Explore aromatic curry preparations inspired by Indian culinary traditions, with a choice of flavours and ingredients.

Vegetarian Dishes & Dals

Discover comforting lentil dishes and vegetarian classics, from richly simmered dals to flavourful vegetable preparations.

Indian Breads

Enjoy freshly prepared Indian breads, including naan and other traditional accompaniments made for sharing with curries.

Rice & Biryani

Rice dishes and aromatic biryani bring fragrant spices and satisfying flavours to the table.

Desserts

Finish your meal with traditional Indian sweets and desserts.
